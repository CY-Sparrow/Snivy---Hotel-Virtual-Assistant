# Snivy - A Simple Virtual Assistence for Small to Medium Hotels

Snivy helps Hotels by saving their time in answering frequently ask questions and getting complaints, booking Issues and Messages from the clients and storing them in database.

## Why this exists?

- Many small and medium Hotels spend most of their time in answering questions which are very comman which can be answer by a virtual assistance like snivy. That saves time for front desk to serve clients else where or to focus on other work. 

- It stores user's general complaints, booking related issues and their messages which can be accessed by hotel's staff member and later can be address by them and connect with the client no need to manually record the issue and maintain an excel sheet.
